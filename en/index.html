Getting here

Find us

A71 motorway, exit 2 Orléans La Source, then the RN20 towards La Ferté-Saint-Aubin. La Ferté-Saint-Aubin railway station is 5 minutes away. Airports: Tours (1h15), Paris-Orly (1h30) and Paris-Charles de Gaulle (2h).
